# Support SLA – Wolf Apps

*Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Rahul Savaikar*

This statement describes the support you can expect for the free Atlassian Cloud apps published by Rahul Savaikar ("we"):

- [Cloud Connector for Jira](./jira-cloud-connector-docs/)
- [Portal Fields for JSM Cloud](./jira-portal-fields-docs/)

It covers how to reach us, when we respond, and how we handle security issues. The same terms apply to every app; where an app differs, its section below says so.

## How to get support

- **Email:** [rahul.savaikar@gmail.com](mailto:rahul.savaikar@gmail.com). Name the app in the subject.
- **Documentation and troubleshooting:** each app's documentation, linked above.

Please include your Jira site address and the exact message you see, including any reference it shows. Each app's **Support** page lists what else helps. Never send pairing codes, exports or attachments with confidential data.

## Support hours

Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00 India Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30), excluding public holidays in Maharashtra, India.

Requests received outside these hours are handled on the next business day.

## Response targets

A response means a reply from a person acknowledging the request and, where possible, a first answer or next step. Resolution times depend on the problem and are not guaranteed.

| Priority | Examples | First response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | The app has stopped working for all users of a site or connection; a suspected security issue | Within 1 business day |
| **High** | A feature doesn't work as documented and there is no workaround | Within 2 business days |
| **Normal** | Configuration questions, a problem with a workaround, unexpected messages | Within 3 business days |
| **Low** | Feature requests, documentation feedback | Within 5 business days |

We set the priority based on the impact you describe; tell us if a problem blocks your work.

## Escalations from Atlassian

Customer escalations forwarded by Atlassian Support receive a first response within **48 hours**, Monday to Friday.

## Security issues

Report suspected vulnerabilities by email with **"Security"** and the app's name in the subject. We acknowledge security reports within 1 business day and fix confirmed vulnerabilities within the timeframes of Atlassian's [Security Bug Fix Policy for Marketplace apps](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/marketplace/security-bugfix-policy/).

## Service availability

Every app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. Its availability depends on Atlassian Cloud and Forge; see [Atlassian Status](https://status.atlassian.com/).

## What this statement does not cover

- Problems caused by Jira configuration outside the app, such as workflows, screens, portal access, permissions or SLA setup
- Custom development or on-site work

## App-specific notes

### Cloud Connector for Jira

- **Critical** includes synchronisation stopping for all issues of a connection.
- Changes that can't be delivered while a partner site is unreachable are retried automatically every hour for up to a day, and administrators can retry them under **Activity → Failed changes**.
- Not covered: the partner's Jira site and its own installation of the app, which its administrators manage.

### Portal Fields for JSM Cloud

- **Critical** includes the report or charts failing for all customers of a portal, and a customer seeing requests they shouldn't.
- Not covered: which fields administrators choose to show customers.
