# Support SLA – Portal Fields for JSM Cloud

*Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Rahul Savaikar*

This statement describes the support you can expect for **Portal Fields for JSM Cloud**, a free app for Atlassian Jira Service Management Cloud. It covers how to reach us, when we respond, and how we handle security issues.

## How to get support

- **Email:** [rahul.savaikar@gmail.com](mailto:rahul.savaikar@gmail.com)
- **Documentation and troubleshooting:** [rsavaikar.github.io/documentation/jira-portal-fields-docs](https://rsavaikar.github.io/documentation/jira-portal-fields-docs/)

Please include your Jira site address, the service project, and the exact message with its reference, if it shows one. Never send exports or screenshots with confidential request data.

## Support hours

Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00 India Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30), excluding public holidays in Maharashtra, India.

Requests received outside these hours are handled on the next business day.

## Response targets

A response means a reply from a person acknowledging the request and, where possible, a first answer or next step. Resolution times depend on the problem and are not guaranteed.

| Priority | Examples | First response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Critical** | The report or charts fail for all customers of a portal; a suspected security issue, such as a customer seeing requests they shouldn't | Within 1 business day |
| **High** | A feature doesn't work as documented and there is no workaround | Within 2 business days |
| **Normal** | Configuration questions, a problem with a workaround, unexpected messages | Within 3 business days |
| **Low** | Feature requests, documentation feedback | Within 5 business days |

We set the priority based on the impact you describe; tell us if a problem blocks your work.

## Escalations from Atlassian

Customer escalations forwarded by Atlassian Support receive a first response within **48 hours**, Monday to Friday.

## Security issues

Report suspected vulnerabilities by email with **"Security"** in the subject. We acknowledge security reports within 1 business day and fix confirmed vulnerabilities within the timeframes of Atlassian's [Security Bug Fix Policy for Marketplace apps](https://developer.atlassian.com/platform/marketplace/security-bugfix-policy/).

## Service availability

Portal Fields for JSM Cloud runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. Its availability depends on Atlassian Cloud and Forge; see [Atlassian Status](https://status.atlassian.com/).

## What this statement does not cover

- Problems caused by Jira configuration outside the app, such as portal access, permissions or SLA setup
- Which fields administrators choose to show customers
- Custom development or on-site work

## Changes

We may update this statement; the date at the top shows the latest version. The [End-user terms](./end-user-terms.html) continue to apply.
