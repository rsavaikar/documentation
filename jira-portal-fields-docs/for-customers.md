# For portal customers

*Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Rahul Savaikar*

Your service provider has added an **Advanced request report** to their customer portal. It shows your requests with more details than the standard list, and lets you sort, filter, export and chart them.

## Opening the report

1. Log in to the customer portal and open **Requests** (from your profile menu, or the Requests link).
2. Under the page title, find **Advanced request report**.
3. Click **Open report** for the list, or **Charts** for charts.

If you don't see the section, your provider hasn't switched it on for the portals you use.

## The request list

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Find a request | Type in **Request contains…**; it searches every visible column |
| Show open or closed requests | Choose **Open requests**, **Closed requests** or **Any status** |
| Choose whose requests | **All requests I can see**, **Created by me**, **Where I'm a participant** or **Shared with my organizations** |
| Narrow to a portal or request type | Use the service project and request type lists |
| Sort | Click a column heading; click again to reverse |
| Open a request | Click its reference, for example SUP-1042; it opens in a new tab |
| Save the list | **Export CSV** or **Export Excel** saves the filtered list, with your columns, to your computer |

The list shows 50 requests per page; use **Previous** and **Next** to move between pages.

## Your columns

Click **Columns** to choose and arrange columns:

- Tick or untick a column to show or hide it. **Reference** always stays, as it opens the request.
- Drag a column by its handle (⋮⋮), or use the arrows, to move it.
- **Reset to default columns** brings back the standard order and shows every column.

Your layout is saved to your account, so it's the same on every computer and browser.

## Charts

**Charts** opens four charts about your requests. Pick one on the left:

| Chart | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Requests by status | How many requests are in each status |
| Created vs resolved | Requests created and resolved per week or month; choose the last 12 weeks, 6 months or 12 months |
| SLA met vs breached | For each SLA, how many were met and how many breached |
| Requests by field | Requests per value of a field you choose, for example priority |

Switch between **Chart** and **Table** to see the numbers. Under **Download**, save the chart as **PNG**, **JPG** or **PDF** (with its filters, date and table), or its numbers as **CSV** or **Excel**.

Not every portal offers every chart or export; your provider decides.

## What you can see

You see the same requests as in the portal's own Requests list: your own, those you take part in, and those shared with your organisations. For some portals, your provider may choose to share all of the portal's requests with every customer. Your provider also chooses which extra fields are shown.
