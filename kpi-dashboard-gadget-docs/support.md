# Support

*Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Rahul Savaikar*

Email [imnicky23@gmail.com](mailto:imnicky23@gmail.com). Support hours and response times are described in the [Support SLA](../sla.html). Many questions are answered in [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.html).

To help us help you quickly, include:

- your Jira site address (for example `yourcompany.atlassian.net`);
- which gadget (KPI Dashboard or Partner Case Overview) and the dashboard it's on;
- the exact message, or a screenshot;
- whether the gadget uses a JQL query or a saved filter, and the field names and prefixes in its settings;
- what you expected to happen and what happened instead.

Please don't send exports or screenshots that contain confidential ticket data.

**Security issues:** report them to the same address with "Security" in the subject.
